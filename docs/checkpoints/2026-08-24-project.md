## User-verified gameplay details added since the prior checkpoint

### Controls and action rules

- Left stick moves and faces; `X` is light attack; `Y` resolves a finisher; `L1` uses the active school’s defence.
- D-pad Up/Down/Left/Right select Fire/Water/Air/Earth.
- Laema cannot move while attacking. Each chained attack may face a new direction without moving her.
- Y without an accumulated X does nothing. Combos accept one through five X inputs followed by Y; a sixth X is ignored.
- Early attack inputs are ignored and not buffered.
- A valid Y ends the combo whether it hits or misses.
- Only one Fire–Water school change is allowed in an active combo. Air/Earth selections are ignored during that combo.

### Prototype animation scope

- X1 through X5 use five different placeholder motions selected from the total combo position.
- School switching preserves that animation position; it does not restart at X1.
- The next attack may snap directly into the new school’s motion; transition animation and pose blending are not required.
- Fire and Water share the same X1–X5 animation set, hitbox, timing, damage, and Impact for now.
- Fire and Water share one Y presentation across both schools and all finisher levels.
- School color and effects communicate the performing school.

### Fire, Water, and layered finishers

- Fire Y applies one DoT stack per resolved Fire level. DoT ticks grant no Heat and cause no flinch.
- Water creates a level-scaled area at weapon–target contact and sends one direct event to each unique Enemy in that area.
- Water levels are:
  - level 1: Wet for 1 second;
  - level 2: Wet for 2 seconds;
  - level 3: 20% Slow for 2 seconds;
  - level 4: 40% Slow for 2.5 seconds;
  - level 5: Frozen for 1 second.
- Wet is reserved for a future Air-lightning interaction and has no active modifier in the current slice.
- Water statuses are mutually exclusive: higher level replaces lower, equal level refreshes without stacking, and lower is ignored while its direct damage still resolves.
- Primary and secondary Fire/Water specialties perform their genuine behavior as separate events in primary-then-secondary order.
- Each direct event that actually reduces Health grants Heat independently.

### Defence

- Fire uses parry; Water uses sustained block. Air/Earth defence is deferred.
- Water block intercepts damage while Impact still reaches hit reaction, depletes guard from blocked damage, drains Heat while held, emits warning feedback, and blocks the breaking hit.
- Guard break uses a provisional 0.5-second reaction; continued L1 hold cannot rearm. Release and repress restore guard and begin a new block.
- Fire L1 opens one parry window. A successful parry stops both damage and hit reaction.
- Holding L1 after the parry window provides no defence while movement remains locked. Release and repress are required to rearm.

### Health, hit reaction, Enemy, and Heat

- Player and Enemy use the same Entity/HealthEvent/HealthResult/HealthResolver/Health/Buff-Debuff/HitReaction foundation.
- Player and Enemy maximum Health are initially 100. Functional Fire/Water X and Y direct damage are initially 10 with Impact 1.
- Final reaction level is `max(0, Impact - defensive level)`.
- Level 0 produces no new reaction. A new nonzero reaction atomically replaces the active reaction.
- A Player reaction cancels the current action, exits defence, resets the combo, locks movement/input, and returns Combat to READY after recovery.
- The Enemy is a stationary, non-attacking permanent target with finite Health. Damage is capped at remaining Health; excess is discarded; reaching zero refills it to maximum without death and without clearing active effects.
- Heat is granted only from unique direct Fire/Water X/Y results that reduce Health. DoT, status, zero-delta, and future healing events grant none.
