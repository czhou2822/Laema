## Current design contract

The current `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` is the **Laema Side-Scrolling Orb Casting Prototype** and records a user-verified design ready for technical handoff.

- Grounded left/right movement, gravity, a continuous flat floor, and horizontal camera following replace the earlier top-down test arrangement.
- All four schools have functional X attacks and casting specialties.
- A chain has five progression positions, at most two schools, and at most one school switch.
- Successful X hits create a FIFO elemental-orb queue.
- R2 pressure now has three transition-based meanings:
  - `0–10%`: DEPLETING;
  - `35–65%`: CHARGING;
  - `90–100%`: CAST on entry.
- Values between those bands retain the previous semantic state. Releasing R2 no longer casts.
- Charging scales with Heat; depletion uses the same fixed baseline rate as zero-Heat charging.
- X and full-press Cast requests share one normalized pre-window buffer. Earliest valid input wins.
- Full-press Cast consumes and freezes marked-orb composition immediately; an interrupted buffered Cast is not refunded.
- Orb lifetime, marking, FIFO transfer, consumption, failed Cast, normal Cast, empowered Cast, mixed-school resolution, and projectile launch are documented in the GDD.
- Prototype school mapping is Fire/Fighter, Water/Prototype Saber Fighter, Air/Shinobi, and Earth/Samurai. This is presentation-only, not final Laema art.
- Jumping, vertical traversal, Air/Earth defence, active Enemy attacks, final art/UI, complete enemy content, and final tuning remain excluded.
