## Locked internal pitch flow

Status: User decision — 2026-08-27. This is a presentation contract, not gameplay or narrative authority.

1. Explain the toy and combat system in detail, demonstrating choice depth rather than button mashing.
2. Bridge with the question: **What kind of game deserves this toy?**
3. Reveal the ground-zero fantasy: walking into a *Heroes of Might & Magic* town and living there, then introduce Laema and the wider game.

### Locked 20-minute allocation

- **0:00–15:00 — Toy (75%):** open with the toy, then demonstrate combat depth through loop, chains, timing, R2 pressure, elemental composition, failure cost, and contrasting player outcomes; conclude that it is a *Might & Magic* system.
- **15:00–16:00 — Bridge:** **“What kind of game deserves this toy?”**
- **16:00–18:00 — Ground-zero fantasy:** town-life fantasy and Laema reveal.
- **18:00–19:00 — Wider-game promise:** concise setting, progression, and narrative material.
- **19:00–20:00 — Close:** the pitch ask.

### Locked reveal

- Open the toy section with: **“You asked for a toy. I began there.”**
- Conclude the combat explanation with: **“It is a Might & Magic system.”**
- Pivot into the wider game with: **“The combat system is how she survives. The town is why every fight matters.”**
- Present Laema as a **candidate** for the next Hero of Might and Magic; do not claim an approved franchise continuation or mandate.

### Locked Q&A positioning — China-market question

Status: User decision — 2026-08-27. This is judge-Q&A material only and must not appear as a main-pitch slide or a provocative comparison with *Black Myth: Wukong*.

- China-first does not require adding Chinese cultural decoration to a fantasy world that does not need it.
- The answer is product confidence: present a credible premium proposition through a recognisable fantasy legacy, clear player fantasy, distinct combat toy, and experience that visibly earns its ambition.
- Laema does not seek to imitate *Black Myth*; it must make its own value legible through a living *Might & Magic* town and a combat system where Might and Magic are equally playable.
