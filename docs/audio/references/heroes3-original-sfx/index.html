Original WAV files are preserved byte-for-byte. The player uses decoded copies with the original sample rate, channels and declared sample length. No normalization, resampling, remixing or new effects are applied. The waveform is a peak overview; it does not alter the sound. Filenames and source-bank identities are preserved without guessing gameplay meanings. Standalone soundtrack MP3s and cutscene audio are outside this sound-bank collection.
