## Results and limits
- X1 R05: stronger eight-pose cut and recovery study with matte materials and neutral hair.
- X4 R02: clearer raised high-thrust study. An optional material variant is included separately.
- X5 R04: stronger nine-pose weight-shift study; R05 contributes only an isolated recovery-body pose. Sword continuity remains unverified.
- X2 r03b: rear-sweep alternative, not an approved direction or hitbox correction. Braking remains understated.
- X3: sword-depth continuity remains unresolved; no replacement selected.

These are not sprite-ready or engine-validated animations. Opaque backgrounds are retained. The preview uses illustrative pose dwell, not actual combat timing. No build, runtime test, hitbox, collision, movement, damage, input or camera changes were made.
