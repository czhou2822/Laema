## Decisions and carried context

- **Decision:** Laema is a local original protagonist, with no personal connection to the royal court. She helps develop Mirham across the campaign.
- **Decision:** Mirham is the recurring buildable hub; Cloudfire is a nearby recurring location, and Steadwick is distinct from both. Cloudfire's people, routines and places become familiar before its fall.
- **Decision:** Laema recognizes symptoms of a poison previously encountered near her town; that poison is later used against King Nicolas Gryphonheart. The investigation and causal connection are not yet designed.
- **Decision:** The late sequence is Cloudfire falls → defenders and survivors retreat toward Mirham → Mirham's final defence → Catherine reaches ruined Cloudfire → the opening of Heroes III: The Restoration of Erathia.
- **Constraint:** This future-game material is outside the active side-scrolling combat/tutorial prototype. It creates no code requirement or acceptance criterion.
